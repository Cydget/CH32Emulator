Static variables

      
Symbols in SRAM from the ELF. Checked variables are sampled while the core runs and drawn on the graph. Choose a break, then Step to break runs until that static increments, decrements, or becomes the value you enter. Step still advances one instruction. Run stops on the same transition. Back reverses one instruction, up to 10 cycles.
